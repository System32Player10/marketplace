Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

1. Overview

1.1 About This Policy. This Privacy Policy explains how MarketPlace collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares information when you use our marketplace, including our website, applications, account features, purchasing services, seller tools, and customer support.

1.2 Your Privacy. MarketPlace understands that privacy is important. We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the platform, provide marketplace services, maintain security, and improve the experience of our users.

1.3 Scope. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through MarketPlace services that link to or reference this Policy.

1.4 Relationship to Other Policies. This Privacy Policy should be read together with the MarketPlace Terms of Service and other policies that govern your use of the platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide. When you create an account or use MarketPlace, you may provide information such as your name, username, email address, telephone number, profile information, shipping information, and other details necessary to use particular features.

2.2 Profile Information. Information you choose to add to your profile may include a profile picture, cover photo, biography, store information, and other optional information. Depending on your settings, some of this information may be visible to other users.

2.3 Marketplace Activity. We may collect information about your activity on MarketPlace, including searches, viewed products, saved products, listings, purchases, orders, reviews, ratings, and interactions with sellers or buyers.

2.4 Transaction Information. When you participate in a transaction, we may collect information relating to the order, transaction amount, payment status, shipping information, delivery status, returns, refunds, and related communications.

2.5 Communications. Information contained in messages, support requests, feedback, reports, and other communications submitted through MarketPlace may be collected when necessary to provide or improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Providing MarketPlace. We use information to create and manage accounts, display products, facilitate purchases and sales, process orders, provide customer support, and operate other features of the platform.

3.2 Improving the Service. Information may be used to understand how users interact with MarketPlace so that we can improve functionality, performance, usability, reliability, and new features.

3.3 Personalization. We may use information such as your marketplace activity and preferences to provide relevant search results, recommendations, notifications, and other personalized experiences.

3.4 Communication. We may send service-related communications concerning orders, account activity, security, customer support, policy changes, and other matters necessary to operate your account.

3.5 Security and Fraud Prevention. Information may be used to detect suspicious activity, prevent fraud, protect accounts, investigate abuse, enforce our policies, and maintain the security of MarketPlace.

3.6 Legal Compliance. We may process information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal obligations, court orders, or valid requests from authorized authorities.

4. How Information Is Shared

4.1 With Other Users. Certain information must be shared between buyers and sellers to facilitate marketplace transactions. For example, sellers may need relevant delivery information to fulfill an order.

4.2 With Service Providers. MarketPlace may work with third-party providers that help us operate the platform, including providers for hosting, payment processing, analytics, communications, security, customer support, and delivery.

4.3 For Legal Reasons. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to respond to legal processes, protect rights, investigate unlawful activity, or comply with government requirements.

4.4 Business Transactions. If MarketPlace is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, relevant information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4.5 We Do Not Intentionally Sell Personal Information. MarketPlace does not intentionally sell personal information to unrelated third parties for purposes unrelated to operating the service, except where permitted by applicable law and appropriately disclosed.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

5.1 Cookies. MarketPlace may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, support essential functionality, and understand how the platform is being used.

5.2 Essential Technologies. Some cookies or similar technologies may be necessary for features such as authentication, security, shopping carts, account sessions, and transaction processing.

5.3 Analytics. MarketPlace may use analytics technologies to understand traffic, performance, feature usage, and general user behavior.

5.4 Managing Cookies. Depending on your browser, device, and applicable law, you may be able to control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some MarketPlace features.

6. Data Retention and Security

6.1 Data Retention. MarketPlace retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements.

6.2 Account Deletion. Where available, users may request closure or deletion of their MarketPlace account through account settings or customer support.

6.3 Information After Deletion. Account deletion does not necessarily mean that all information is immediately erased. Certain information may need to be retained for legal, security, accounting, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

6.4 Security Measures. MarketPlace uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, destruction, and loss.

6.5 Security Limitations. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While MarketPlace takes reasonable precautions, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

7.1 Account Information. You may review and update certain account information through your MarketPlace account settings.

7.2 Access Requests. Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to certain personal information held by MarketPlace about you.

7.3 Correction Requests. You may request correction of personal information that you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

7.4 Deletion Requests. Where applicable law provides such a right, you may request deletion of certain personal information, subject to legal and operational exceptions.

7.5 Communications. You may be able to manage optional notifications and promotional communications through your account settings or the controls provided in individual messages.

7.6 Verification. To protect your information, MarketPlace may need to verify your identity before completing certain privacy-related requests.

8. Special Circumstances

8.1 Children's Privacy. MarketPlace is not intended for individuals who are not legally permitted to use the service under applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

8.2 International Processing. Depending on our infrastructure and service providers, information may be stored or processed in countries other than the country in which you live. Such processing will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

8.3 Third-Party Services. MarketPlace may link to or integrate with services operated by independent third parties. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms, which govern information they collect directly.

8.4 Public Information. Information that you voluntarily make public, such as product listings, seller profiles, reviews, and public comments, may be viewed and accessed by other users.

9. Changes and Contact Information

9.1 Changes to This Policy. MarketPlace may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, business practices, or legal requirements change.

9.2 Last Updated Date. The "Last Updated" date displayed at the beginning of this Policy indicates when the current version was most recently revised.

9.3 Notice of Material Changes. Where appropriate, MarketPlace may provide additional notice of significant changes through the platform, email, account notifications, or other reasonable communication methods.

9.4 Questions and Requests. If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, you may contact MarketPlace through the official support channels provided on the platform.

9.5 Effective Date. Changes to this Privacy Policy become effective on the date specified in the updated version unless otherwise stated.
